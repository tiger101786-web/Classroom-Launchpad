# Fire and Ice

Full-size lossless PNG assets: fire-v1.png, fire-v1-underlay.png, ice-v1.png, ice-v1-underlay.png in this directory. Existing original and Storm assets are unchanged.

Created using the built-in image-editing tool. The original tiger.png provides anatomy, silhouette and placement; the user's Molten Lava Saber-Tooth Tiger.png provides the dramatic elemental styling. Earlier subtle variants were discarded, not shipped.

## Final prompt direction

Edit the original tiger's coat only. Preserve its broad face, rounded eyes and expression, proportions, ears, cheek tufts, fangs, all four paw/claw positions and canvas registration. Fire: obsidian-black and deep red fur split by intense yellow-white molten fissures, glowing orange-gold fur edges and orange irises. Ice: deep glacial sapphire and teal-blue fur split by brilliant cyan-white frozen fissures, crystalline frost on fur strands, silver-white edges, luminous ice-blue irises and pearlescent icy sabers/claws. Match the dramatic intensity of the supplied lava reference. No anatomy redesign, external spikes, particles, platform or text. Full square framing, transparent background, sharp detailed fur.

## Animation-layer prompts

Use original limb-underlay.png as locked geometry and the selected elemental coat as color reference. Repaint only coat colors, markings and eye color. Keep belly, rear legs and every rear claw unchanged; do not add front legs. Match the visible coat seamlessly. For Fire's foreground, explicitly retain the original's two front legs and front paws while transferring the generated molten underlay's coloring.

Generated images are not claimed to have byte-identical silhouettes. The original animation mesh and timings are unchanged. Verify foreground/underlay pairs at native resolution and visually in idle, paw, sleep and dance states. Production tests include both skins, saved selection after reload, the unchanged 2048px render surface and Scrapbook hat contrast.
