# Decorative scene frames

Created with the built-in image-generation tool as transparent PNG overlays. Original alpha preserved. These are separate from the stationary scene artwork and do not intercept clicks.

## Assets and design prompts

- `assets/scene-frame-blossom.png`: Cherry Blossom: slender rose-gold hoop wrapped in delicate sculpted branches, pale pink five-petal blossoms and burgundy leaves, largest clusters upper left and lower right.
- `assets/scene-frame-guardian.png`: Dragon Guardian: bronze and deep emerald scaled circular hoop, one small elegant sculpted dragon head at upper left looking outward, subtle horns, folded wing at upper right and amber gemstones, tail curling around bottom.
- `assets/scene-frame-woodland.png`: Enchanted Forest: intertwined natural wooden vines and moss-green leaves, small mushrooms at bottom left, tiny golden firefly jewels along rim, magical handcrafted woodland ornament.
- `assets/scene-frame-orbit.png`: Cosmic Explorer: deep blue and polished silver circular hoop, golden stars along rim, crescent moon at upper left, a small purple ringed planet at upper right, tiny blue gemstone constellations along bottom.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game profile frame overlay, square 1024x1024 PNG with genuine transparent alpha background and fully transparent empty center. [Design prompt above.] Premium detailed dimensional illustrated collectible frame, polished materials, school-friendly. Geometry: front-on perfectly circular hoop centered at image center (512,512), hoop centerline radius 435 pixels. Keep the inner circle radius 405 completely empty and transparent. All decorative art confined to annulus radius 405 to 500. Narrow elegant ring, ornaments close to rim, no part cut off. No filled disk, no scene, no portrait, no text, no watermark, no background or checkerboard painted into image, no perspective tilt. Outer corners AND central circular hole transparent. Keep lower-right 4-to-5-o'clock segment thin to leave room for a settings control. Single standalone frame asset.
