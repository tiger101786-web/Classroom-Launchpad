Classroom Launchpad

Privacy & Student Data

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Purpose

Classroom Launchpad uses Google sign-in to keep Colt Corner and other protected classroom features available only to approved St. Cletus students and the teacher.

Information used

When a user signs in, the site receives the name, email address, verified-account status, Google account identifier, and school Workspace domain provided by Google. The site does not receive or store Google passwords.

How information is used

Identity information is used only to confirm classroom access, display the verified author of a topic or reply, support teacher moderation, and prevent impersonation. Classroom posts and website requests are stored so the shared classroom features can work.

Storage and sharing

Approved-email records and classroom content are stored in the private server data area. Student email lists are not published through the website or placed in the public site code. Information is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Access and removal

The teacher can add or remove approved accounts and moderate classroom content. Students or families may ask the school or teacher to review or remove applicable Classroom Launchpad information.

Google account data

The site requests only the basic identity information needed for sign-in. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendars, or files.

Security

The server verifies Google’s signed identity token, checks the school domain and private approved-email list, and uses secure login cookies. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but access controls are designed to prevent unauthenticated visitors from reading protected classroom content.
