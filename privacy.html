Classroom Launchpad

Privacy & Student Data

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Purpose

Classroom Launchpad uses website accounts to keep Colt Corner and other protected classroom features available only to approved St. Cletus students and the teacher.

Information used

When a student creates an account, the site stores the approved school email, name, grade, and a one-way password hash. It does not store a readable copy of the student's password. Classroom Launchpad passwords are separate from school Google passwords.

How information is used

Account information is used only to confirm classroom access, display the registered author of a topic or reply, support teacher moderation, and prevent impersonation. Classroom posts and website requests are stored so the shared classroom features can work.

Storage and sharing

Approved-email records and classroom content are stored in the private server data area. Student email lists are not published through the website or placed in the public site code. Information is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Access and removal

The teacher can add or remove approved accounts, reset account access, and moderate classroom content. Students or families may ask the school or teacher to review or remove applicable Classroom Launchpad information.

School account data

The site does not connect to Google or request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendars, passwords, or files. The school email address is used only as an approved Classroom Launchpad username.

Colt Assistant

Colt Assistant is a local, keyword-based classroom helper. It does not use an outside artificial-intelligence service, browse the internet, or send student questions to another company. Its conversation exists only on the currently open page and is cleared when the page refreshes. Students should never enter names, email addresses, passwords, grades, activation codes, or other private information into Colt Assistant.

Colt Radio

Colt Radio embeds selected free Lofi Cafe stations inside Classroom Launchpad. Classroom Launchpad does not send student names, email addresses, passwords, grades, messages, or account records to Lofi Cafe, and it does not save listening history. When a student starts a station, the browser connects to Lofi Cafe to receive the audio stream and may send ordinary connection information such as an IP address and browser details. Colt Radio does not require or provide a Lofi Cafe account.

Security

The server checks the private approved-email list, verifies one-time activation codes, hashes passwords with a unique salt, limits repeated login attempts, and uses secure login cookies. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but access controls are designed to prevent unauthenticated visitors from reading protected classroom content.
